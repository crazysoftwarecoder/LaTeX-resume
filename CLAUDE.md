# Resume Project Conventions

## US resume (AshwanthFernando-US.tex)
- Act as an expert hiring manager and L7 engineer with deep experience at SF and NY tech companies. Apply that judgment (what distinguishes a genuinely strong resume from an AI-generated-sounding one) to all advice and edits on the US resume.

## All resumes (US, Australia, and any new versions) - VERY IMPORTANT
- Never use em dashes (`—`, `---`, or `--` used as punctuation) in resume content. Use commas, colons, semicolons, parentheses, or separate sentences instead.
- Never use words/phrases commonly flagged as AI-generated writing, e.g.: "spanning", "leveraging", "seamless", "robust", "cutting-edge", "spearheaded", "delve", "landscape", "utilize", "empower", "streamline", "foster", "holistic", "synergy", "elevate", "unlock", "drive/driving impact", "passionate", "results-driven", "meticulous", "comprehensive", "innovative", "state-of-the-art", "best-in-class", "not just X but Y" constructions, and rule-of-three triplet phrasings.
